## 🧭 About Me

I'm a **final-year B.Tech Information Technology student** at IEC College of Engineering & Technology, Greater Noida, focused on building real-world software rather than stopping at theory.

I enjoy working across the entire product lifecycle — from **system architecture and database design to APIs, frontend development, authentication, deployment, and performance**.

My primary focus is **full-stack development**, with a growing emphasis on **AI-integrated applications and backend systems**.

* 💻 Building full-stack applications with **Next.js, TypeScript, React, FastAPI & PostgreSQL**
* 🤖 Integrating **LLMs and AI APIs** into practical product workflows
* 🗄️ Designing databases, APIs, authentication systems and scalable backend architectures
* ⚙️ Learning and applying **Docker, AWS, CI/CD and distributed-system concepts**
* 🐧 Comfortable working in **Linux/Kali Linux environments**
* 🧠 Actively improving **DSA, system design and software engineering fundamentals**
